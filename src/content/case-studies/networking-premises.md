The premises needed more than stronger Wi-Fi. Staff needed reliable coverage, visitors needed guest access, cameras needed a clean network path, and trusted remote access had to be controlled rather than improvised.

## The Challenge

The existing setup had grown in pieces. Wireless coverage was inconsistent, guest access was not clearly separated, and camera traffic was part of a wider network that needed tidying. The business also wanted a better way to support remote access without exposing more than necessary.

## What Centrix Delivered

Centrix reviewed the site, user needs, device types, cabling, switching, and remote access requirements. The design separated staff, guest, camera, voice-ready, and management traffic using practical segmentation and firewall rules.

The project also included VPN access planning, camera network considerations, and clear documentation for future support. The result was a network that could support the current premises and future changes.

## Outcome

The site gained clearer Wi-Fi coverage, cleaner network separation, controlled access paths, and a simpler CCTV viewing experience. The handover documentation made the network easier to manage and extend.
