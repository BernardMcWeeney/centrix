The team wanted to understand where AI could help everyday work, but they were also concerned about data protection, accuracy, and staff using tools without guidance.

## The Challenge

AI was already visible to staff, but the business did not yet have a shared understanding of suitable tools, safe prompts, sensitive data boundaries, or realistic use cases. The project needed to cut through hype and focus on work the team actually performs.

## What Centrix Delivered

Centrix ran a plain-English introduction covering ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, prompt quality, data handling, and workflow examples. The session connected AI ideas to real tasks such as drafting, summarising, reporting, customer response, and internal knowledge work.

The follow-up produced a shortlist of use cases, policy points, and pilot candidates that could be tested without exposing sensitive information.

## Outcome

The business left with practical AI use cases, safer staff guidance, and a controlled path for pilots. Staff had a clearer view of where AI helps, where it does not, and what should not be entered into public tools.
