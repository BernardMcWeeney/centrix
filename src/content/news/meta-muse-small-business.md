Meta has announced [Muse for Small Business](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/), extending its personal agent with business skills and connections to tools such as Canva, Shopify, Slack and accounting services. It can also connect to Facebook and Instagram business accounts.

Meta says publishing, sending and spending require approval. The announcement describes Muse’s initial availability in the US and Canada; Irish businesses should check regional access rather than assuming an immediate local rollout.

## Why connected agents need a plan

The opportunity is to reduce the time spent moving information between business applications. The same connections also create decisions about access to customer records, financial information and communications.

Centrix’s practical takeaway is to define a narrow task, review each permission and retain human approval over external actions. Demonstrations can use synthetic information while the business assesses supplier terms and data flows. A promising product announcement alone is not approval to connect live client systems.
