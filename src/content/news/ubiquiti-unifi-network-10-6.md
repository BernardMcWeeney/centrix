Ubiquiti’s [25 August Network 10.6 announcement](https://blog.ui.com/article/introducing-unifi-network-10-6) describes improvements for managing larger networks, with attention to topology analysis, automation, security and high availability insights.

The direction matters for organisations with multiple switches, access points or sites: troubleshooting depends on understanding how those devices work together and what changed before a fault appeared.

## Plan the upgrade around the site

Centrix recommends checking the official release notes against the gateway, switches and access points actually installed. Confirm the supported upgrade path and take a configuration backup before scheduling a maintenance window.

After upgrading, verify business traffic rather than relying only on a successful update message. Check staff Wi-Fi, guest isolation, VPN access and any services that depend on VLANs or firewall rules. Record the result so the next change starts from a known working configuration.
