The Department of Justice, Home Affairs and Migration opened a consultation on the draft National Cyber Security Strategy on 9 July 2026. Its [departmental announcement, reproduced by Public Technologies](https://ebs.publicnow.com/view/074A77DE2E294ADCC608410A85FC3BE0E736356F), describes proposals to address systemic cyber risks while supporting digital development and innovation.

The announced consultation period ran until 21 August 2026 at noon and invited participation from people living in Ireland. This article covers the consultation milestone; it does not describe the draft as an adopted final strategy.

## Why businesses should follow it

National resilience also depends on everyday business decisions: who can access systems, how suppliers are assessed, whether backups can be restored and how incidents are escalated.

Centrix’s practical takeaway is to document those responsibilities now. A clear asset list, named incident contacts and tested recovery arrangements are useful foundations while organisations follow the strategy’s development and assess their own sector requirements.
