The Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment’s [27 May strategy update](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-enterprise-tourism-and-employment/policy-information/digital-and-ai-strategy/) explains the ambitions of *Digital Ireland – Connecting our People, Securing our Future*. It brings digital innovation, AI adoption and public service improvement into a national programme looking towards 2030.

The department highlights support for businesses through Enterprise Ireland, Local Enterprise Offices and CeADAR, Ireland’s National AI Hub. These are useful starting points for organisations exploring an initial AI project.

## What this means for Irish businesses

Centrix’s practical takeaway is to choose a defined business problem, agree how success will be measured, and check the information involved before selecting a tool. A small pilot with clear ownership gives management a better basis for investment than an unrestricted rollout.
