Meta has introduced [Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/10/introducing-muse-wonder-models/), building on the Muse Spark family with a focus on personal and professional agent tasks.

The announcement makes Muse Wonder available through the Meta Model API and Muse Code, with Muse Wonder Full available to Muse Code subscribers. Meta says Wonder will subsequently power more Muse experiences, beginning with subscribers. Its described features include Active Memory for relevant context across conversations and Active Focus for following longer tasks through setbacks.

## What to assess before adoption

These are Meta’s product claims and should be tested against the intended workflow. Centrix’s practical takeaway is to evaluate completion quality, recovery from errors and approval points alongside the headline model capability.

For an agent that can work across documents or applications, define its permitted access and how that access can be revoked. Confirm regional availability and provider terms before committing to deployment with business data.
