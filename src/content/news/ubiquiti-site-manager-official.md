Ubiquiti has made the [new Site Manager](https://blog.ui.com/article/officially-bringing-unifi-fabrics) official, with a focus on managing multiple UniFi sites through a unified Fabric model.

The announcement covers central visibility, granular roles, third-party identity provider integration, zero-touch deployment, blueprint-style orchestration, Canvas-based design, and API-driven workflows.

## Why It Matters

For businesses with several locations, the management problem is often consistency. Wi-Fi names, firewall policies, admin access, VLANs, alerting, and documentation drift over time when each site is treated separately.

Site Manager's direction is useful for organisations that want a repeatable branch-office model. The important operational work remains the same: define a standard, test it at one site, document exceptions, and use central tools to keep future changes controlled.
