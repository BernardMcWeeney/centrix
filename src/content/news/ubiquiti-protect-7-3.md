Ubiquiti’s [Protect 7.3 announcement](https://blog.ui.com/article/introducing-protect-7-3) introduces options to keep recent footage at higher quality and older footage at reduced quality, alongside Super Encoding to improve storage efficiency.

Teleport allows supported remote cameras to record to a central UniFi Cloud Gateway or NVR without an additional console at the camera location. Time Machine playback is available from video walls, with further changes to sensor and identification management and a Shopify integration.

## Plan recording around the business need

For multi-site businesses, these features may simplify deployment and incident review. Centrix recommends checking camera compatibility, connection capacity and recorder requirements before making changes.

More available storage should prompt a review of the recording policy. Define the required image quality, who can view or export footage and how long recordings need to be retained. Storage efficiency should support a documented retention requirement rather than automatically extending it.
