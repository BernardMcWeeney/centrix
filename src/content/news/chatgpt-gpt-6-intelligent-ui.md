OpenAI has announced [GPT-6 with Intelligent UI](https://openai.com/index/gpt-6-for-everyone/) for ChatGPT. Answers can combine text, diagrams, comparisons and interactive elements that help people explore information directly in a conversation.

The announced rollout begins with Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise in Chat, followed by Free and Go. Paid plans use GPT-6 Sol and Free and Go use GPT-6 Luna. Workspace settings can affect Enterprise access. This Chat release does not replace the models powering Work and Codex.

## What Irish businesses can try

Centrix sees useful pilot tasks in staff learning, explaining technical concepts and comparing options with clearly stated assumptions. Interactive answers can make those activities easier to explore, while the underlying facts and calculations still need review.
