ENISA’s [30 September announcement](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/news/cybersecurity-month-work-employees-skills-under-the-looking-glass) sets the workplace context for European Cybersecurity Month. The October campaign promotes awareness across Europe, with ENISA and the European Commission supporting activities led by Member States and other organisations.

Its message is practical: digital workplaces need staff who can recognise risks and know how to respond. Training and preparedness deserve attention alongside technical controls.

## Make October useful for your team

Centrix suggests four short activities that a small business can complete during the month:

- Practise reporting a suspicious message using the actual internal reporting route.
- Review how payment-detail changes are independently verified.
- Check that staff know how to report a lost device or unexpected sign-in prompt.
- Run a short recovery exercise with the people responsible for backups and incident decisions.

Keep a record of what was learned and assign an owner to any gaps. Awareness becomes more valuable when employees can act confidently during a real incident.
