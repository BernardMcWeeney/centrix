Cloudflare has published [how it built Town Lake and Skipper](https://blog.cloudflare.com/our-unified-data-platform/), describing the data platform and internal AI agent that sit behind parts of its analytics workflow.

The post is a useful reminder that AI agents are only as good as the data, permissions, retrieval paths, and operational controls underneath them.

## Why It Matters

Many businesses want an AI assistant before they have clean data access, ownership, or reporting. Cloudflare's example points in the opposite direction: build a reliable data layer, then let AI operate against known systems.

For practical deployments, the starting point is often data hygiene, permissions, and workflow mapping rather than a model decision.
