Ireland launched its [National Cyber Security Strategy 2030](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-justice-home-affairs-and-migration/publications/national-cyber-security-strategy-2030/) on 7 October 2026. Following the previous strategy published in 2019, it addresses systemic cyber risks and sets a direction for protecting Ireland’s digital infrastructure and strengthening national resilience.

The strategy combines protection of society and essential infrastructure with support for innovation, Ireland’s cyber security industry and cooperation with European and international partners.

## Three pillars and 29 measures

The strategy is organised around three pillars:

- **Detect and Defend:** develop Ireland’s ability to identify and respond to cyber threats.
- **Enhance National Resilience:** strengthen the country’s capacity to withstand and recover from cyber incidents.
- **Strengthen Our Cyber Ecosystem:** support the skills, research and industry needed for lasting cyber security capability.

Its 29 measures include developing a National Detection Network, establishing a National Cyber Hub and developing new National Cyber Defence Services. It also sets out full implementation of new EU cyber security legislation relating to critical infrastructure and products with digital elements, and the establishment of a Cyber Security Research Centre of Excellence.

Read the [full National Cyber Security Strategy 2030 (PDF)](https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/3c7e136d/National_Cyber_Security_Strategy_2030.pdf) for the measures and their wider context.

## Informed by consultation

A targeted consultation in April 2026 received 13 submissions from academia, industry and public sector bodies. A public consultation launched in July received 241 submissions, including 80 with detailed input. Themes included emerging technology risks, support for the Irish cyber security industry and public sector recruitment challenges.

The [consultation summary report (PDF)](https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/44ae0f25/Consultations_on_the_National_Cyber_Security_Strategy_-_Summary_Report.pdf) explains the feedback considered during drafting.
