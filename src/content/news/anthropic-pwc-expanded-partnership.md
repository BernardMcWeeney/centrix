Anthropic and PwC have announced [an expanded strategic partnership](https://www.anthropic.com/news/pwc-expanded-partnership), with PwC rolling out Claude Code and Claude Cowork, creating a joint Center of Excellence, and training tens of thousands of professionals.

The partnership focuses on agentic software delivery, AI-native deal work, finance transformation through an Office of the CFO business group, and production deployments across sectors such as insurance, healthcare, HR, cybersecurity, and mainframe modernization.

## Why It Matters

The announcement shows enterprise AI shifting from pilots into operating model change. PwC is presenting Claude as part of client delivery and internal transformation, not just as a productivity assistant.

For businesses evaluating AI, this reinforces the importance of choosing a small number of high-value workflows first, measuring delivery impact, and building governance around the work before widening access.
