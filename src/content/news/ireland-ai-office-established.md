The [AI Office of Ireland announcement](https://aioffice.gov.ie/news-and-updates/ai-office-of-ireland-established-under-the-ai-regulation-bill-2026-paul-byrne-appointed-as-ceo/) confirms the establishment of the independent statutory office and Paul Byrne’s appointment as its first Chief Executive Officer.

The office will coordinate Ireland’s approach to implementing the EU AI Act, work with sectoral regulators and provide a central point of contact. The announcement also identifies support for responsible AI innovation and adoption as part of its role.

## A practical governance step

For businesses, a useful first step is an inventory of AI tools and their purposes. Record who owns each use, what data it can access, which supplier provides it and whether someone reviews consequential outputs or actions.

Different uses carry different responsibilities. A drafting assistant and an AI system involved in recruitment should not be assessed as though they were the same deployment. Follow the office’s guidance and obtain advice specific to the intended use where needed.
