Microsoft has published [highlights from its 2026 Digital Defense Report](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/01/insights-from-the-2026-microsoft-digital-defense-report/), examining an increasingly interconnected threat environment.

The report discusses AI use by attackers in reconnaissance, social engineering, malware and exploit development, and activity after compromise. Microsoft notes that much of this use currently supports parts of existing attack workflows.

## Keep the response grounded

Centrix’s practical takeaway is to revisit the controls that interrupt those workflows: strong authentication, limited administrator access, timely updates, monitored endpoints and recoverable backups.

Businesses should also review supplier access and remote management tools. An incident can involve more than one organisation, so response planning needs named contacts and a clear way to disable access quickly.

Use the report to inform priorities, then assess the systems and exposures relevant to your business. Vendor research provides context; it does not by itself establish an individual organisation’s level of risk.
